The catalog lists Leishmania genome assemblies and the metadata of each: accession or identifier, species, strain, assembly level, assembly statistics, file checksums and provenance (BioProject, BioSample, Zenodo record). It does not host sequences: leishref install downloads a genome from the source its entry records (NCBI, TriTrypDB or a Zenodo record) into a local cache.

By source

NCBI: assemblies with a GCA_ or GCF_ accession. Custom: assemblies added by hand to the catalog, with a Zenodo record where they are published. NCBI-Nucleotide: a single NCBI sequence that is not an assembly (for example a lone kinetoplast sequence). TriTrypDB: a genome taken from TriTrypDB. Leishref: a scaffold derived locally from another assembly.

Known limits

  • Only assemblies recorded in the catalog are listed; it is curated, not a mirror of NCBI.
  • Most entries record a FASTA only. A GFF is recorded for the few that have one, and Leishgene can only search those.
  • Statistics and technology labels come from the metadata of each entry and may be missing for older assemblies.
